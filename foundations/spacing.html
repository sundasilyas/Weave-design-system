Spacing

A 4px spacing system that keeps every layout aligned, balanced and consistent.

Spacing system

Weave uses a 4px spacing system. Every space, padding and gap is a multiple of 4, so layouts line up and feel consistent. Fewer choices means faster decisions and less guesswork.

Each small square is 4px; the darker lines are every 16px. The card's padding (16px), gaps (12px and 4px) and text line heights are all multiples of 4, so every edge lands on a grid line.

Text rhythm

Space creates rhythm and shows how ideas are grouped: 16px between paragraphs, 24px above an H3 or H4 and 8px below it, and 48px above an H2. Lines stay within 60–75 characters. The blue bands show the spacing.

Writing for small screens

A few habits that make interface text easier to scan, read and act on.

Good interface text gets people to the right place quickly. It uses plain words, puts the most important information first and leaves out anything that doesn't help.

People rarely read every word. They scan for the part they need, so short paragraphs and clear headings help them find it.

Lead with what matters

Put the outcome or the action first. "Save your changes before leaving" is clearer than a sentence that builds up to the point.

If something can go wrong, say what happened and what to do next, in that order.

Keep labels short

Buttons and labels work best as one to three words that describe exactly what happens.

Test with real people

Read your text out loud, then watch someone use it. Wherever they hesitate, the words can probably be simpler.
