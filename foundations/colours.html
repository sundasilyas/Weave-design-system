Use these colours in this order for charts and graphs: Data 1 for the first series, Data 2 for the second, and so on. Never rely on colour alone. Add labels, patterns or shapes so people can tell the series apart without seeing colour.

Accessibility

Good contrast lets everyone read our products, not only people with perfect eyesight on a perfect screen. Around 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women have a colour-vision deficiency. Many more people read on old or dim screens, in bright sunlight or low light, or with eyesight that changes with age. If we design for enough contrast from the start, the product works for all of them.

Minimum contrast

WhatMinimum ratio
Body text (under 18px)4.5:1
